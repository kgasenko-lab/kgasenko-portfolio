Music School

Enrollment & Operations Transformation

The project began as a search for school-management software. The evidence showed that the more urgent constraint was earlier in the journey: families could not easily understand, compare and enroll in programs.

Why the software request was not the first priority

DDefine

The assignment began as a search for school-management software. Enrollment, scheduling, payments and communication were consuming administrative time, and a new platform appeared to be the obvious solution.

MMeasure

I reviewed the full family journey—from discovering a program to comparing schedules, booking a trial and enrolling—and assessed which information staff had to recreate across pages, messages and internal records.

  • Program, age, schedule and tuition information was difficult to compare.
  • Trial and registration paths created avoidable questions and drop-off.
  • The school lacked a consistent acquisition foundation to evaluate software against.

AAnalyze

The immediate constraint was not the back-office platform. Families first needed a clearer way to understand and choose the right program. Replacing software before fixing that journey would have reorganized administration without addressing enrollment.

IImprove

I changed the sequence: improve acquisition first, then select the system using requirements proven through the redesigned operation.

  • Rebuilt program pages, schedules, pricing, trial and enrollment paths.
  • Created reusable email, campaign and SEO foundations.
  • Mapped operational requirements and evaluated candidate platforms.

CControl

Standardized content and enrollment assets made updates easier to maintain. The operating-requirements map created a practical scorecard for hands-on platform testing rather than a feature-led buying decision.

RResult

The same programs, launched at comparable enrollment timing, produced a much stronger first month while the school entered the technology phase with evidence-based requirements.

Results

  • ~4×First-month registrations versus the prior-year comparison.
  • ~4×First-month revenue for the same programs and timing.
  • 5Platforms shortlisted for hands-on operational testing.
  • One clear journeyProgram discovery, trial and enrollment brought into a consistent path.

TBest Staffing Agency

Candidate Acquisition & ATS Operating-System Reset

The business needed more candidates and clearer recruiting performance. The request looked like an ATS replacement; the real work was rebuilding the operating system around it.

From an ATS problem to an operating-system reset

DDefine

The business needed a larger, more reliable candidate flow for bulk hiring and clearer visibility into recruiting performance. The initial request focused on replacing an ATS that recruiters found difficult to search and reuse.

MMeasure

I established the operating baseline across acquisition, database quality and recruiter work.

  • Up to roughly 300 resumes per month required continuous promotion.
  • The website produced almost no organic resume flow.
  • Boolean search, parsing, integrations and duplicate/DNR tracking were weak.
  • Selected database searches took hours instead of minutes.

AAnalyze

The ATS was only one part of the constraint. Candidate acquisition, referrals, website visibility, record quality, recruiter workflows, reporting and adoption formed one interconnected system. A software-only replacement would have transferred the fragmentation.

IImprove

I rebuilt the operating ecosystem around real recruiter work.

  • Compared platforms across more than 90 operational criteria.
  • Tested end-to-end recruiter workflows before selection.
  • Implemented and configured a low-cost ATS, CRM and workforce stack.
  • Trained users and built source, pipeline and performance reporting.

CControl

Configured workflows, shared data rules, user training and leadership reporting made the new process repeatable. Searchable records became a reusable business asset rather than a collection of isolated applications.

RResult

Candidate flow increased while dependence on continuous promotion fell, and recruiters could reuse a substantially larger database with faster, more consistent searches.

Results

  • 2–3×Candidate flow with limited promotion.
  • ~1.5×Candidate flow without active promotion.
  • 20K+Searchable candidate records available for reuse.
  • Hours → minutesTime required for selected Boolean searches.

Online CME Systems

End-to-End Digital Delivery System

Healthcare education programs had to move online and support thousands of participants without becoming dependent on an expensive all-in-one platform.

Building the operation behind the online event

DDefine

The visible goal was to move physical CME events online and support a much larger audience without buying an expensive all-in-one platform.

MMeasure

I mapped the participant lifecycle and found separate manual processes for promotion, registration, payment, access, attendance, testing, certification, recordings and follow-up. Physical programs typically supported roughly 100–500 participants.

AAnalyze

Digitizing each task independently would have recreated the same fragmented operation on a screen. The unit of design had to be the complete participant journey, including the data generated at every stage.

IImprove

I designed and built the connected delivery system myself using a near-zero-cost technology stack.

  • Connected promotion, registration and payment to event access.
  • Integrated attendance, testing, certification and recordings.
  • Built more than 50 multilingual landing pages.
  • Connected participant data, reporting and follow-up across programs.

CControl

Reusable page patterns, data flows and delivery workflows supported repeat events without rebuilding the operation each time. Central reporting and participant records reduced dependence on vendors and isolated spreadsheets.

RResult

The system supported thousands of simultaneous participants and converted one-time event records into a growing professional audience that could be reused across future programs.

Results

  • 1K–4KParticipants supported at one time.
  • 4K → 45K+Growth of the professional participant database.
  • 50+Multilingual landing pages connected to delivery.
  • Near-zero costTechnology stack built to limit vendor dependency.

Healthcare Marketing & CME Agency

Audience Acquisition & Registration Transformation

Event growth depended on clinic visits, paper invitations and handwritten registration. Improving attendance meant redesigning acquisition and data capture together.

Turning registration into a reusable acquisition system

DDefine

The organization needed to grow attendance across a large annual CME calendar. Promotion depended on clinic visits and paper invitations, while event-day registration remained handwritten.

MMeasure

I traced the work from invitation to follow-up and documented repeated manual effort, limited geographic reach, duplicate data entry and errors in names, email addresses and participant records.

AAnalyze

Registration was being treated as an administrative event-day task. In practice, it was the center of audience acquisition and the source of data needed to improve promotion, attendance and future programs.

IImprove

  • Created event landing pages and online registration paths.
  • Expanded promotion beyond one-clinic-at-a-time outreach.
  • Structured participant-data capture and follow-up.
  • Connected each event to a reusable professional audience.

CControl

Standard registration fields and repeatable digital workflows improved record consistency. The growing audience could be segmented and reused rather than rebuilt from paper lists for every event.

RResult

The agency gained a repeatable acquisition and data workflow capable of supporting 80–100 events a year with cleaner participant information and broader digital reach.

Results

  • 80–100Events supported annually.
  • Manual → digitalPromotion and registration workflow.
  • Cleaner dataStructured participant records replaced handwriting and re-entry.
  • Reusable reachAudience retained across programs instead of rebuilt each time.

Operating model · Customer behavior · Service design

Hotel & Restaurant Complex

Service & Operating Model Redesign

Internal event services were selling poorly. The apparent sales problem was actually a mismatch between client behavior, employee coverage and ownership of the relationship.

Redesigning the service around how clients made decisions

DDefine

Internal event services were underused, and revenue was leaving the business. The initial assumption was that managers needed to sell the additional services more actively.

MMeasure

I examined the customer decision journey, handoffs, manager schedules, service ownership and commission structure.

  • Banquet and event-service decisions were owned by different managers.
  • Clients often made decisions during evenings and weekends.
  • Standard schedules created gaps at high-intent moments.

AAnalyze

The sales issue was an operating-model mismatch. Customers had to adapt to internal departments and availability, creating late handoffs, repeated conversations and opportunities for outside vendors.

IImprove

  • Combined banquet and event-service responsibility across the journey.
  • Aligned commission with the complete client relationship.
  • Introduced two-manager coverage on a three-days-on, three-days-off rotation.
  • Matched availability to the times clients actually made decisions.

CControl

Clear ownership and overlapping coverage reduced dependency on a single manager. The rotation maintained evening and weekend availability while preserving predictable recovery time.

RResult

Internal service adoption and vendor-fee revenue increased substantially, while clients experienced fewer handoffs and managers gained a more sustainable coverage model.

Results

  • ~90%Banquet events adopting internal event services.
  • ~4–6×Increase in monthly vendor-fee revenue.
  • One ownerRelationship managed across banquet and event services.
  • 3 on / 3 offTwo-manager rotation expanded coverage sustainably.
