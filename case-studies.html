Customer journey · Digital operations · Business systems

Music School

Enrollment & Operations Transformation

The project began with a request for school-management software. The evidence showed a more immediate business priority: families first needed a clearer path to understand and enroll in programs.

Visible request

Reduce manual administration

Enrollment, scheduling, payments, communication and student updates consumed too much time.

Operational evidence

The parent journey was fragmented

Programs, age groups, schedules, tuition, trials and registration paths were difficult to compare and maintain.

Root cause

Software was not the first constraint

Implementing a platform first would not solve the immediate enrollment and revenue problem.

Implemented change

Fix acquisition before migration

Rebuilt program pages, schedules, pricing, trial and enrollment paths, email assets, SEO foundations and campaign materials. Then mapped requirements and shortlisted five systems for hands-on testing.

Measured result

~4× first-month growth

Registrations and revenue increased approximately fourfold compared with the same period the previous year—with the same programs and enrollment timing.

Business systems · Operations · Process transformation

Staffing Agency

Candidate Acquisition & ATS Operating-System Reset

The company needed more candidates and better visibility into recruiting performance. Replacing the ATS alone would have moved the bottleneck rather than solve it.

Visible request

Increase candidate flow

The existing process produced up to approximately 300 resumes per month but required ongoing promotion and was insufficient for bulk hiring.

Operational evidence

Search and reuse were weak

The ATS limited Boolean search, parsing, storage, integrations and duplicate/DNR tracking. The website generated almost no resume flow.

Root cause

The surrounding operation was fragmented

Candidate acquisition, database reuse, reporting, referrals, website visibility and recruiter workflows all affected the outcome.

Implemented change

Rebuilt the operating ecosystem

Compared platforms across 90+ criteria, tested recruiter workflows, implemented a low-cost ATS/CRM/workforce stack, configured processes, trained users and built comprehensive reporting for strategic planning.

Measured result

2–3× candidate flow

Flow increased approximately 1.5× without active promotion and 2–3× with limited promotion. A 20K+ searchable database and improved Boolean workflows reduced selected searches from hours to minutes.

Digital transformation · Automation · Event operations

Online CME Systems

End-to-End Digital Delivery System

Healthcare education programs needed to move online without becoming dependent on an expensive all-in-one platform. I built the system myself using a near-zero-cost stack.

Visible request

Move CME events online

Physical programs supported hundreds of people; the new environment needed to support thousands.

Operational evidence

The participant lifecycle was disconnected

Registration, payments, communication, access, attendance, testing and certification operated as separate tasks.

Root cause

The operation—not only the event—needed redesign

Moving every manual task online separately would reproduce the same fragmentation digitally.

Implemented change

Built one connected delivery system

Created 50+ multilingual landing pages and connected registration, payments, access, live delivery, testing, certification, recordings, participant data, reporting and follow-up.

Measured result

1K–4K participants at one time

Capacity grew from roughly 100–500 physical attendees. The professional database grew from approximately 4K to 45K+ while manual work and third-party dependency decreased.

Event operations · Digital acquisition

Healthcare Marketing & CME Agency

Audience Acquisition & Registration Transformation

Before the larger online transformation, event acquisition depended on clinic visits, physical invitations and handwritten registration.

Visible request

Increase event attendance

The operation relied on staff visiting clinics, delivering invitations and manually following up.

Operational evidence

Paper limited reach and data quality

Handwritten registration required repeated data entry and introduced errors in participant names, emails and records.

Root cause

Registration was part of acquisition

It was not only an event-day task; it determined audience reach and the quality of the reusable database.

Implemented change

Created a digital acquisition path

Introduced event landing pages, online registration, digital promotion and structured participant-data workflows.

Operating result

Manual → digital

Promotion and registration expanded beyond clinic-by-clinic outreach, supporting a reusable digital audience across approximately 80–100 events annually.

Operating model · Customer behavior · Service design

Hotel & Restaurant Complex

Service & Operating Model Redesign

Internal event services were selling poorly. The obvious response was to push the team to sell more. The actual problem was how client ownership and coverage were designed.

Visible request

Increase event-service sales

Banquet and additional event services were handled separately by managers working limited standard schedules.

Operational evidence

Coverage did not match customer behavior

Clients preferred evenings and weekends. Late handoffs introduced decision fatigue and lost sales.

Root cause

Fragmented ownership—not effort

The model expected customers to adapt to the business rather than organizing the service around how they planned events.

Implemented change

One owner across the journey

Combined banquet and event-service responsibilities, aligned commissions and introduced two-manager coverage on a 3-days-on / 3-days-off rotation.

Measured result

~4–6× vendor-fee revenue

Approximately 90% of banquet events adopted internal event services. Coverage improved while managers gained stronger commission opportunities and better rest balance.
