My approach

Diagnose first. Change second.

I structure case studies using DMAIC logic, with Results separated so the business impact is easy to scan.

D · DEFINE

What was

Clarify the visible business need, starting condition and expected outcome.

M · MEASURE

Baseline

Measure volume, time, cost, conversion, errors, workload and constraints.

A · ANALYZE

What I found

Find the root cause—including behavior, handoffs, incentives, system gaps and process friction.

I · IMPROVE

What I changed

Redesign the process, system, customer journey, operating model or decision logic.

C · CONTROL

What it became

Make the improved workflow repeatable through systems, reporting, standards and adoption.

R · RESULTS

Business impact

Show the measurable effect on growth, efficiency, quality, cost, capacity or adoption.

How I work

Behavior + systems + measurable change.

I pay attention to how people actually behave inside a process: where customers stop, where employees bypass a workflow, where handoffs fail and where a system adds friction instead of removing it.
