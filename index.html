TBest Staffing Agency

The brief was to increase candidate flow and replace an underperforming ATS. The deeper issue was a fragmented recruiting operation: acquisition, database reuse, search, reporting and adoption did not work as one system.

Up to ~300 resumes a month, weak website conversion and hours-long database searches.
What I changed
Evaluated platforms across 90+ criteria, implemented an ATS/CRM stack, redesigned workflows and trained recruiters.
Business result

How I work

Diagnose first. Change second.

I use DMAIC logic as a practical operating discipline: understand the situation, establish evidence, identify the constraint, redesign the work and verify that the change delivers a better result.

  1. 01
    Understand the operation

    Map what people, customers and systems are doing now.

  2. 02
    Establish the baseline

    Use data and observable behavior to define the starting point.

  3. 03
    Find the real constraint

    Separate the visible request from the cause limiting the result.

  4. 04
    Redesign the workflow

    Clarify ownership, sequence, information and system requirements.

  5. 05
    Implement with people

    Configure, communicate, train and support the change in practice.

  6. 06
    Measure what changed

    Compare the outcome with the baseline and adjust where needed.
