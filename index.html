My focus

Business transformation, technology adoption, process improvement, data-informed decisions and practical change implementation.

My approach

Start with the problem. Prove the cause. Change what matters.

My project logic is based on Lean Six Sigma DMAIC, adapted for business transformation so the process remains clear to any hiring manager or client.

Find the root cause, including behavior, system gaps and process friction.

How I work

Behavior + systems + measurable change.

I pay attention to how people actually behave inside a process.

I am interested in why customers stop, why employees bypass a workflow, why a system is not adopted, and why a process that looks correct on paper does not work in practice.

That perspective has shaped my work across customer journeys, marketing, ATS implementation, digital event systems, operations and technology adoption.
