The diagnostic difference

I do not start with the tool.

I start with what people are doing, where the process breaks, what the data shows and which constraint matters first.

Sometimes the answer is a new platform. Sometimes it is a different priority, clearer communication or a redesigned operating model.

How I work

Diagnose first. Change second.

The work follows DMAIC logic without forcing every project into textbook language. The business question stays visible at every step.

  1. 01Understand

    What is happening now?

  2. 02Measure

    What does the baseline show?

  3. 03Find the cause

    Why is it happening?

  4. 04Redesign

    What needs to change?

  5. 05Implement

    How will people use it?

  6. 06Measure again

    Did it actually work?
