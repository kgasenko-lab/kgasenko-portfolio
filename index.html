When to bring me in

You know something needs to change. The right solution is not obvious yet.

01

The problem is visible, but the cause is not.

You may already be considering new software, automation, hiring or more marketing. I help determine whether that is actually the right place to start.

02

Your operation has grown around workarounds.

Too many spreadsheets, manual steps, duplicate data, unclear ownership or disconnected tools are slowing the team down.

03

The process looks logical, but people do not behave the way it expects.

Customers stop, employees bypass steps, or teams create their own ways of getting the work done.

04

You are about to make a significant operational or technology investment.

I can help define requirements, compare options and test them against real workflows before the business commits time and money.

05

The new process is live, but adoption is weak.

Technical implementation may be complete while the operational change is still unfinished.

Why my approach is different

I do not start with the tool.

I start with what people are actually doing.

I look at the process, the data, the handoffs, customer or employee behavior, business constraints and the systems supporting the work.

Then I decide what needs to change.

Sometimes the answer is a new platform. Sometimes it is a different workflow, clearer communication, a better operating model or a change in priorities.

About

I like problems that are not obvious at first.

I have worked across staffing, healthcare education, events, digital operations and small-business transformation.

The industries changed. The pattern did not.
